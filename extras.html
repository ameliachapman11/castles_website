Extended commentaries and supplements

As explained in the book, my long-term intention is to provide extended commentaries on each chapter along with supplements on various specific topics, for the benefit of readers wanting to delve into the material in greater depth.

The completion of all of these will be a major undertaking. Meanwhile, the draft versions below may contain material of interest. Those marked (*) are in a fairly finished state — the others may require expansion or revision and may not be quite in sync with the actual contents of the book. I hope to add more commentaries and supplements soon, and reserve the right to update these drafts at any time.
